Belts have NUMBER_OF_POSITIONS_PER_TILE positions, when straight.
Belts have responsibility to position items on themselves from -ITEM_SPACING / 2 to POSITIONS_PER_TILE - ITEM_SPACING / 2.
When placed in lanes, items always have non-negative positions.
Items move from high positions towards zero.
Information about how far items shold move moves from zero to higher positions.
